Core Courier Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026. Replaces the policy of 16 September 2026.

Core Courier is a single-player arcade game. The game itself collects nothing about you. It offers one optional rewarded video ad, served by Google AdMob, and that ad is the only part of the app that uses the network or handles any information. This policy explains both.

What the game itself stores

Your best scores, any shields you have banked, and a run you left in progress are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They never leave the device and are removed when the app is uninstalled. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and the game plays offline.

The optional ad

You can choose to watch a short video ad to earn a shield that absorbs one hit. Nothing in the game is locked behind an ad, and a player who never watches one plays the same game. The ads are provided by Google AdMob and are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

To show ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects the following and shares it with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location.
  • How you interact with the app and the ads, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use.
  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This information is encrypted in transit. Core Courier's developer does not receive it and cannot see it. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires consent, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for it through Google's consent message before any ads are requested. You can change your answer at any time with Privacy choices on the title screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings under Google, Ads.
  • You can simply never watch an ad.

Children

Core Courier is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If what the app collects changes, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
