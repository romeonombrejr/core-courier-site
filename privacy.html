Core Courier Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026

Core Courier is a single-player arcade game. This policy explains what the app does with information, which is very little.

What the app collects

Nothing. Core Courier does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no network access of any kind. The app does not request the internet permission.

What the app stores on your device

Your best score is saved in the app's private storage on your device so it can be shown to you on the next launch. It never leaves the device and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Third parties

The app includes no third-party software development kits and shares no information with anyone. Google Play may collect information about your download and use of the app under Google's own privacy policy; that is separate from this app and outside its control.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no information, it collects no information from children.

Changes

If the app ever begins to collect information, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: corecourier.dev@gmail.com
